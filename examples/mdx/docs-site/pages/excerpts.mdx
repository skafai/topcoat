# Excerpts

Excerpts let you split a document into a summary and full content. Everything in this first section serves as the excerpt, suitable for listing pages or search results.

---

The rest of the document appears after the thematic break. This is the full article content that would only be shown when the reader navigates to the individual page.

Excerpts are computed at compile time by the MDX processor. The separator itself renders as a horizontal rule in the output.
